# Project Build Room company starter

## Company pitch

Kinship Business Banking is a fictional company in London. It opens business accounts for family-run businesses. It does not claim a real banking licence and it does not lend. The customers are Metalwharf Grocers and Canada Circle Lettings. One must never see the other's records.

Your company starts here. You're its only security engineer.

## Set up (5 minutes)

1. Click **Use this template > Create a new repository** at the top of this page.
2. Name it after your company and choose **Private**.
3. Open your new repo in your AI tool. In Claude Code, the Founder skill loads automatically from `.claude/skills/founder/`; in other tools, paste `.claude/skills/founder/SKILL.md` in as instructions.
4. Ask: "Use the Founder skill to interview me and found my company." Save the result in `company.md` and paste the story into your Confluence space.
5. Open `docs/org-chart.drawio` and `docs/architecture.drawio` in draw.io and make them yours.

## What's here

| File | What it is |

## Rules

- Fake data only: never real people, customers or your employer's systems or details.
- Never commit secrets, keys or passwords.
- Keep your repo private until you've checked it's safe to show.
